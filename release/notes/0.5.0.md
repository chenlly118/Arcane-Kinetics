# Arcane Kinetics 0.5.0 Beta / 奥术动力学 0.5.0 测试版

Minecraft **1.21.1** · **NeoForge 21.1.251+** · **Java 21** · 客户端与服务端均需安装 / install on both client and server.

## 中文

- **机械法术：**应力驱动命中生物时会使其旋转。鼓风熔炼可处理缠魂和烟熏配方；命中生物时施加短暂的减速和失明，正常施法推开目标，潜行施法将其吸向施法者。
- **新增机器：**烟花反应装置可发射烟花；将两个传送门漏斗配对，即可远程传送物品，支持跨维度。
- **机器操作：**黑洞反应堆控制器采用原版容器风格的界面；奥术施法装置支持六向摆放；用扳手可让卷轴印刷机切到卷轴升级模式。
- **法术加工与工具：**可用指定法术命中材料完成配方。魔化扳手可在主手或副手使用：潜行上滚切换为扳手，下滚切回施法用的法杖。模式随物品保存。
- **柴油动力联动（可选）：**用原油与法力混合得到奥术燃料，分馏后得到精炼奥术燃料和墨水。精炼燃料提供柴油两倍的应力容量；大型发酵罐可升级墨水。
- **巫术学联动（可选）：**统一兼容的流体和奥术板。奥术板采用 `create_spellbook:arcane_plate`，旧巫术学奥术板迁移时保留数量和物品数据。
- 游戏内启用「奥术动力学」名称与聚魔盆图标；奇点锭和奇点块采用更深的黑紫色。

## English

- **Mechanical spells:** Stress Drive spins creatures on hit. Fan Smelting processes haunting and smoking recipes, briefly slows and blinds creatures, and pushes them away or pulls them toward the caster while sneaking.
- **New machines:** The Firework Reactor launches fireworks. Pair two Portal Funnels to transfer items remotely, including across dimensions.
- **Machine controls:** The Black Hole Reactor controller has a vanilla-style interface. Arcane Casting Arms can face all six directions. Use a wrench on the Scroll Printer to switch to scroll upgrade mode.
- **Spell processing and tools:** Cast the specified spell on materials to complete a processing recipe. While holding the Enchanted Wrench in either hand, sneak and scroll up for wrench mode or down for staff mode. The item remembers its mode.
- **Optional Create: Diesel Generators integration:** Mix crude oil with mana to make Arcane Fuel, then distill it into Refined Arcane Fuel and ink. Refined fuel supplies twice the stress capacity of diesel. Use a Bulk Fermenter to upgrade ink.
- **Optional Create: Wizardry integration:** The mods share compatible fluids and the Arcane Plate. Legacy Wizardry plates migrate to `create_spellbook:arcane_plate` with stack counts and item data intact.
- The Arcane Kinetics name and Focusing Basin icon are now shown in game. Singularity Ingots and Blocks use a darker violet tint.

Create: Diesel Generators、Create: Wizardry 和 JEI 均为可选联动 / all three integrations are optional. [安装与依赖 / Installation and dependencies](https://github.com/chenlly118/Arcane-Kinetics#安装环境)
